# Video Chops Studio

## Register

product

## Users

People editing local video clips at a desktop, starting with files in `data/input/` and saving finished videos in `data/output/`.

## Product Purpose

Preview a source video, trim clip boundaries, split and remove sections, adjust a crop directly on the frame, and export the remaining clips as one MP4. All video processing runs locally.

The Two regions layout selects two rectangles from one source and arranges them on an output canvas. Users choose a common aspect ratio, move and shrink the regions, and optionally add a background color, local image, or frame. Source and output previews stay synchronized, and export uses the same layout.

## Brand Personality

Calm, compact, practical. Carry over the visual language of the adjacent `carousell-card-creator-studio` app.

## Design Principles

- Keep the source, preview, timeline, and export settings within one workspace.
- Show what will be kept before exporting it.
- Preserve original files and never overwrite an existing export.
- Make precise edits possible with both direct manipulation and numeric inputs.
- Describe loading, progress, empty states, and failures in plain language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use labeled controls, visible keyboard focus, sufficient text contrast, keyboard alternatives to dragging, and reduced-motion support.
